01 / Research Questions & themes

Strong gravity, from geometry to data.

My research follows the full chain from relativistic theory and numerical calculation to signatures that can be tested with black-hole images and gravitational-wave observations.

  1. 01

    Black-hole phenomenology

    Horizons, shadows, photon dynamics, gravitational lensing, rotating solutions, and precision tests of gravity in the strong-field regime.

  2. 02

    Gravitational waves

    Black-hole perturbations, quasinormal modes, ringdown spectroscopy, waveform structure, and observable signatures beyond general relativity.

  3. 03

    Numerical relativity & quantum information

    Computational spacetime dynamics and information-theoretic viewpoints that connect geometry, evolution, and the structure of gravitational systems.

  4. 04

    AI-assisted inference

    Neural posterior estimation, normalizing flows, and simulation-based Bayesian inference for expensive relativistic models and joint observables.

03 / Methods Computation & inference

Tools for difficult inverse problems.

The work combines analytic reasoning with numerical experiments and statistically principled inference, with an emphasis on reproducible pipelines.

Relativistic dynamics Perturbation theory, waveform calculations, and numerical spacetime evolution.
Observable modeling Ray tracing, photon dynamics, black-hole shadows, jets, and ringdown signals.
Bayesian inference Posterior estimation and uncertainty-aware interpretation of multi-modal observations.
Machine learning Normalizing flows and neural surrogates for fast, reusable simulation-based inference.
