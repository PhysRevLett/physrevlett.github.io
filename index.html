Research

Understanding strong gravity through observable signatures.

I am interested in how black-hole spacetimes encode new gravitational physics in shadows, ringdown signals, jet observables, and other strong-field phenomena. A parallel thread of my work develops computational tools that make inference from expensive relativistic simulations faster and more reusable.

01

Black-hole phenomenology

Shadows, photon dynamics, lensing, rotating solutions, and strong-field tests of gravity.

02

Gravitational waves

Black-hole perturbations, quasinormal modes, ringdown, and beyond-GR signatures.

03

AI for gravity

Neural posterior estimation, normalizing flows, and simulation-based Bayesian inference.

04

Numerical methods

Ray tracing, waveform calculations, parameter inference, and scalable computational pipelines.
